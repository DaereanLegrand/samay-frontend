## Findings / residuals

1. **Universal-link platform files are deployment placeholders** — the
   Apple `apple-app-site-association` / Android `assetlinks.json` and the
   associated domain live in DEPLOYMENT-PLACEHOLDER.md; until real domains
   exist, the https fallback only matches in dev.
2. **Invite-token spam** is inherent (token-shaped links can be crafted) —
   bounded by: tokens must resolve to real events, the event must be visible
   to the caller (RLS), and invite acceptance goes through `event_invites`
   RLS. Rate limiting for token lookups joins the S-10 Kong work.
3. When the QR invite tokens land (EINV/QEDG), validate the QR RPC's
   `max_failed_qr_validations` enforcement (app_config, seeded — S-10
   handoff).
