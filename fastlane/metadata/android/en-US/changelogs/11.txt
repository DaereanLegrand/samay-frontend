Games-first home: the app opens on the card deck (drag to cycle, tap play).
Choose how many are playing, one name per seat, and save the group to reuse it.

New in this build:
- Charadas: phone on the forehead, tilt down to score and up to pass, with
  Peruvian categories and a turn length you choose.
- Botella: a real spinning bottle on a pizza board.
- Leaderboard of your pass-the-phone games, kept on the device.
