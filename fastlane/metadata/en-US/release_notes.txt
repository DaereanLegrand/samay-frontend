Games-first home: the app opens on the card deck (drag to cycle, tap the play
button). Pick how many are playing, type one name per seat, and save the group
to reuse it next time.

New in this build:
- Charadas: hold the phone on your forehead, tilt down for a point and up to
  pass, with Peruvian categories (animals, pop culture, literature, art,
  sports, TV, food) and a turn length you choose.
- Botella: a real spinning bottle on a pizza board.
- Leaderboard of your pass-the-phone games, kept on the device.
